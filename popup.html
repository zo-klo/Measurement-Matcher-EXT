Your measurements

All measurements are optional and use inches. Leave unused fields blank. Missing listing measurements are skipped. At least one entered, applicable measurement must be available to establish a match. Enter smaller/larger allowances as positive amounts, or 0 for no allowance.

Inseam, rise & leg opening
Inseam (optional)

Rise (pants)

Leave measurement blank to ignore it.

Pants, jeans, and trousers only; applied when reported. Uses rise/front rise, not back rise.

Leg opening (pants)

Leave measurement blank to ignore it.

Pants, jeans, and trousers only; applied when reported. Enter the listing’s inch value; no width/circumference conversion is made.
