# Measurement Matcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026. Applies to Measurement Matcher version 1.9.2.

Measurement Matcher helps you compare clothing measurements on TheRealReal and save checked listing observations for CSV export. It is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TheRealReal.

## Information the extension handles

You can enter waist, bust, hip, inseam, rise, and leg-opening measurements and separate smaller/larger matching allowances for each measurement, in inches. The extension uses these values to compare garments with your chosen range. These values describe physical body measurements; the extension does not diagnose health conditions or provide medical advice.

When matching runs on TheRealReal, the extension reads product pages and saves observations: product URL and available item ID, date/time checked, a random scan identifier and the page URL where that scan started, garment bust, waist, hip, inseam, rise, and leg opening, the saved measurements and directional allowances used, measurement differences, which optional measurement rules were applied, and match result. When extractable, observations also include listing price and currency, sale status, designer, estimated percentage off retail, fabric composition, and garment condition. Saved URLs and dates form a history of checked listings, including products fetched from results pages that you did not open individually. This is not a record of your browsing on other websites.

Recognized bags, shoes, and accessories categories are skipped without product-measurement requests or new observations. This also applies to recognized non-clothing product links in mixed searches. Previously saved observations remain in the stored history until you clear them or uninstall the extension.

Observations also record the garment category when recognized and whether the bust comparison was applied. Bust preferences remain saved when browsing bottoms, where bust is not applicable.

## Purpose and storage

Data is used only to provide measurement matching and your checked-listing CSV history. Sizing preferences are saved with Chrome's storage.sync API. If Chrome sync is enabled, Chrome may send those preferences to Google's synchronization service and make them available in your other synced Chrome browsers. Google handles that synchronization under its own terms and privacy policies. With sync disabled, Chrome stores those preferences locally.

Checked listing observations are saved using Chrome's storage.local API on this Chrome installation. They are not synced by the extension. Observations include the measurements used at the time of checking, so changing your preferences does not edit older observations. Local storage is subject to Chrome's quota. The extension does not add its own encryption to saved data or exported files.

## Network requests and sharing

The extension requests TheRealReal pages over HTTPS to read product details. These same-origin requests use your existing browsing session and may include the session cookies that Chrome normally sends to TheRealReal. The extension does not read, copy, or store cookie values, account passwords, or payment information. Like other website requests, TheRealReal receives request URLs and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Your entered body measurements are compared inside the extension; they are not sent to TheRealReal by the extension.

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or remote code. It does not transmit your saved measurements, listing history, or CSV files to the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or a database service. Google may receive synced preferences as described above; TheRealReal receives normal page requests.

When you choose Export checked listings, the extension creates a CSV and asks Chrome to download it to your chosen download location. The CSV includes the measurements used and checked product URLs. You control any later sharing or database import. If your download folder is backed up or synced by another application, that application's settings and policies apply.

## Access, retention, and deletion

Open the popup to view or change your saved sizing preferences. Leave an unused measurement blank and use Save & apply to save the change. Export checked listings downloads your saved observations without deleting them.

Clear checked listings removes saved listing observations from this Chrome installation after confirmation. It leaves sizing preferences and already-downloaded files in place. Open TRR pages can save new observations as matching continues. To stop new checks, close those pages or disable the extension. Records are otherwise retained without automatic expiry, subject to storage limits, until you clear them or uninstall the extension. Removing the extension clears its Chrome local storage. Remove any downloaded CSV files separately using your device's file controls. Chrome manages synced preferences through its sync settings.

Failed or access-denied product requests are not saved as garment observations. Listing details can be missing or inaccurate if the website changes. Review exported data before relying on it.

## Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility.
